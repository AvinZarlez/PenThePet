How to Play

Objective

Build walls to pen in your pet! The goal is to create the largest enclosed area possible.

Gameplay

Scoring

  • The game checks if your pet can escape (reach the edge)
  • When penned (✓), your area size shows the enclosed space
  • Try to match or exceed the goal area size
  • Larger penned areas score better!
  • You can only submit once per puzzle - plan carefully!
  • After submission, you can view the optimal solution to compare

Hint System

  • When a pet is penned, you can press the Check if Optimal button to tell you whether your current score is the optimal solution.
  • The score display will be yellow 🟡 if below `<` the optimal score. It will turn green 🟢 when the correct answer is found `✅`.
  • If enabled, after checking the first hint, you can get further help by pressing Reveal Target to see the optimal solution alongside your current score.
  • You can disable hints entirely from the Options menu.

Options

You can customize your pet type and hint preferences in the Options menu. Your preferences are saved automatically.

About Pen the Pet

Pen the Pet is a logic puzzle game where you fence in your pet by strategically placing walls to create the largest enclosed area.

Each day features a new hand-crafted puzzle with a unique layout and challenge.

Features

  • Daily puzzles with unique maps
  • Multiple pet types to choose from
  • Configurable hint system
  • Accessible keyboard navigation
  • Mobile-friendly design
